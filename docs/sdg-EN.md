## Motivation

### 1. Why is sustainable development important to me or our team?

Sustainability is important to our team on many fronts, such as:

- Allowing future generations decent quality of life on the planet, just like we had.
- Better professional environments and work conditions for employees.
- Sustainability ties directly into profitability and supporting local communities.
- Increases efficiency and promotes optimization and automization.

---

## Subject

### 2. Why is this software necessary? Does it promote a Sustainable Development Goal?

Our software promotes the following Sustainable Development Goals:  
Goal 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth  
Goal 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

By creating an easy to use and sophisticated inventory software, we can improve the infrastructure and functionality of the business, as well as improving the work quality and efficiency of the business employees. Ideally our software can also be used as a benchmark for future development and innovation in inventory applications.

## Implementation

### 3. How is energy consumption minimized?

**SDG12: Responsible Consumption and Production**

<!-- How essential are the features and contents of the software? -->

Features and Content: The software is an essential inventory system for an instrument rental. It improves inventory keeping, renting, and the workflows of employees.

<!-- Does the software harbour only data that is necessary? -->

Data Storage: The inventory software stores relevant item records, active rental information, past rental information (up to a predefined/required legal timefame).

<!-- How is the server capacity required by the software taken into account? -->

Server Capacity: The software is operated only by the employee, so servers will only run within business hours, and will be powered down for evenings, weekends, and holidays.

<!-- How does the software or its development utilise artifical intelligence? -->

AI usage: The software itself does not provision any artificial intelligence, but AI will be utilized during software development.
